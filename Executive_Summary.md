## Pareto Analysis — Menu Risk

- **42 products** generate **80%** of total revenue
- **38 products** generate only **20%** of revenue
- Menu has **80 items** — too many for operational efficiency

**Action:** Consider reducing menu to 55-60 items by eliminating consistently low-performing products.

---

## Key Observations

**1. Size Matters**
Regular (Rg) and Large (Lg) sizes dominate sales. Small sizes and products without size labels consistently underperform.

**2. Volume Does Not Equal Revenue**
Earl Grey Rg is the most sold product (4,708 units) but ranks only 26th in revenue ($11,770). Price per unit matters as much as popularity.

**3. Chocolate Opportunity**
Drinking Chocolate has only 1 product type — Hot Chocolate. Adding new chocolate flavors could grow this category significantly.

**4. Coffee is the Backbone**
Barista Espresso drinks (Latte, Cappuccino) contribute 33.86% of all coffee revenue. Consistent quality and staff training here is critical.
